01 / About

About me

A researcher focused on applying intelligent computational methods to practical networking and cybersecurity challenges.

I am Ajayanand K., a PhD Scholar in Computer Science at SNS College. My academic and research interests lie at the intersection of computer networks, cybersecurity and artificial intelligence.

My work explores machine-learning-based intrusion detection, reinforcement-learning-driven network optimization, and adaptive mechanisms for improving the security, efficiency and resilience of next-generation networks.

I am interested in research that connects algorithm design with reproducible experimentation and practical network environments.

04 / Selected work

Research projects

Selected research and development directions in intelligent network systems.

Reinforcement Learning-Based Dynamic Firewall Optimization

An adaptive firewall framework combining anomaly detection with reinforcement-learning-based policy decisions to improve network threat response.

DQN · LSTM-CNN · NSL-KDD · CICIDS2017 · SDN

CNN-LSTM-Based Network Intrusion Detection

A hybrid deep-learning approach for learning spatial and sequential traffic patterns, with comparative evaluation against conventional machine-learning models.

CNN-LSTM · Deep Learning · Intrusion Detection

Intelligent Congestion Control for 5G/6G Networks

A proposed research direction investigating deep reinforcement learning for adaptive congestion control, anomaly detection and resource coordination across network slices.
